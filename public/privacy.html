1. Who is responsible

{{site_name}}, Harare, Zimbabwe, decides how your personal information is used on this website (we are the "data controller"). You can contact us about anything on this page using the details at the bottom.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need:

  • Before you buy tickets: your name and WhatsApp number, your email if you choose to give it, and whether you ticked the box to get news about future events.
  • Event updates sign-up: your name, WhatsApp number and/or email, the topics you picked and the fact that you agreed to receive updates (with the date and wording you agreed to).
  • Merch orders: your name, phone number, email (optional unless we need it for a receipt), city, delivery address, any note you add, the number you pay from and what you ordered. Payments are processed by Paynow; we never see your PIN.
  • Visits: we count visits to pages and where visitors came from (for example Instagram). This is done without cookies and without keeping your IP address or anything that identifies you.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or social media.

3. Why we use it

  • Ticket buyers: to send you entry details for the event you are going to and tell you about changes, such as a new time or venue. This is in our and your legitimate interest in the event going smoothly.
  • News and offers: only if you ticked the box saying yes. You can withdraw your agreement at any time, and it doesn’t affect anything else.
  • Merch orders: to take payment, deliver your order and help if something goes wrong. We need these details to carry out the order.
  • Running the site: to keep the website secure, prevent abuse and understand which pages are useful.
  • The law: to keep sales records where tax or other laws require it.

We never sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for automated decisions about you.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the service providers who help us run things, and only what they need:

  • Our ticketing partner (currently Megaticket), when you buy tickets on their site. You give your details to them directly, under their own privacy policy.
  • Paynow and your mobile money provider, to process shop payments.
  • Delivery people or companies, who receive your name, number and address to deliver your order.
  • Website hosting and storage providers (such as Cloudflare and Neon). Their servers may be outside Zimbabwe; where that happens we use providers with strong security and protect your information as this policy describes.
  • The police or other authorities, if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

  • Ticket and update contacts: until you ask us to remove you, or two years after we last heard from you, whichever comes first.
  • Shop orders: as long as tax and accounting law requires (normally six years), then deleted.
  • Visit counts: kept as totals only.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your details, or stop sending you news (you can also just reply “STOP” on WhatsApp);
  • stop using your details for a particular purpose.

Contact us using the details below and we will reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Authority, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ).

7. Keeping it safe

Your information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who need it. Our admin area is password protected. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that puts your information at risk we will act quickly and tell you and the authorities where the law requires.

8. Cookies and device storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no third-party trackers on this site. The only cookie we set is a login cookie for our own admin team.

To make the site work, your browser’s own storage on your phone or computer remembers a few things for you: what is in your shopping bag, the delivery details you typed at checkout, whether you have already given your details before buying tickets, and whether you have already seen the event updates box. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address to send the font files.

9. Children

Our events and this website are meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children; if you think a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a big change to how we use your information we will say so on the website.
