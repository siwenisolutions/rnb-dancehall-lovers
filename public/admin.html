      Google

      To show up on Google: open Google Search Console, add https://www.rnbdancehalllovers.co.zw/ as a "URL prefix" property and choose "HTML tag". Paste the tag (or just its code) here and save, then press Verify in Google. After that, add the sitemap: sitemap.xml.
