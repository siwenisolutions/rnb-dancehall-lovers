What happens if an event is cancelled, moved or changed, and how to return merch or get your money back.

1. If an event is cancelled

If we cancel an event and don’t move it to a new date, you are entitled to a full refund of the ticket price. Refunds are made through our ticketing partner (currently Megaticket) to the way you paid, and we will tell ticket holders how and when on WhatsApp.

2. If an event is postponed or moved

If an event moves to a new date or a different venue, your ticket stays valid for the new date or venue. If you can’t make the new date, contact us or our ticketing partner within 14 days of the announcement and we will arrange a refund.

3. Line-up and other changes

Artists, DJs and set times can change for reasons outside our control. We will always tell you about important changes. If a change is significant (for example, the main act advertised for the event can no longer appear), contact us and we will discuss a refund or another fair solution with you.

4. If you can’t go

If you bought a ticket and can no longer attend, please contact us or our ticketing partner as soon as possible. Whether a ticket can be refunded or transferred to someone else depends on the ticket type and the ticketing partner’s terms shown when you bought it. Tickets sold through anyone other than our official ticketing partner may be fake and can’t be refunded by us.

5. Merch: returns

  • Faulty, damaged or wrong item: tell us within 30 days of delivery and send us a photo. We will replace the item or give you a full refund, and we cover the cost of collecting it.
  • Change of mind: you can cancel your order and return an item within 7 days of receiving it for a refund of the item price. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition and packaging. Unless the item was faulty or wrong, you may be asked to cover the cost of sending it back.
  • Wrong size: we’re happy to swap it for another size while stock lasts, on the same conditions as a change of mind.

6. Merch: how refunds are paid

Once we have received the returned item (or the photo, for a faulty item), we refund you within 14 days to the same EcoCash or InnBucks number you paid from, or another way we agree with you. To start a return, contact us with your order reference (it starts with RDL).

This policy doesn’t take away any of your rights under the Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44].
